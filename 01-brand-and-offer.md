### Visual direction

Aesthetic thesis: a career tool, not a coach's mood board. The site should feel like software (Motion-style), because the product is systems. No pastels, no script fonts, no beige.

**Color palette**

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | #0D1017 | Dark hero and section backgrounds, footer, primary text on light |
| Paper | #F7F8FA | Light section backgrounds |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards |
| Signal gradient | #5B4FF0 to #16B8A6 | The one accent. CTAs, key stats, underlines, badges. Used identically everywhere. |
| Slate | #5A6272 | Secondary text, captions |
| Line | #E4E7EE | Card borders, dividers |

Rule: the gradient is the only decorative color. If something needs emphasis and it is not the gradient, use weight and size instead.

**Typography**

| Role | Face | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Headlines | Space Grotesk, 600-700 | Bold geometric sans. Tight tracking on large sizes. |
| Body + UI | Inter, 400-600 | Clean, high legibility at small sizes. |
| Prompt text / data | JetBrains Mono | Used inside prompt-card mockups only. Signals "this is a real prompt you can copy." |

**Recurring visual devices**

- Prompt cards: content and results shown as clean white cards with soft shadows, styled like an AI chat window with monospace prompt text. This is the signature element.
- Bento grid for breaking down offer components and content pillars.
- Trust bar under the hero: results, student counts, or press once available. Until then: audience count + "built in public" stats (real numbers only).
- One CTA per page, repeated down the page, always in the gradient.

### Brand voice guide (the one paragraph)

Write like a sharp friend who has already done the thing you are scared of. Lead with warmth, then get specific: a real number, a real prompt, a real deadline, never a vibe. Say the direct thing in the first two lines, then structure the rest so it can be skimmed: short paragraphs, headers, bullets. No hype words, no manufactured urgency, no em dashes (use commas, colons, or periods). If a sentence could appear in anyone else's career content, cut it or add the specific detail that makes it mine.

## PART 2: FIRST PAID OFFER

### The offer

**Name:** The Career OS Challenge
**Format:** 14-day live challenge (10 working days of lessons, weekends off)
**Price:** $197 founding cohort (first 50 seats), $297 standard after
**Guarantee:** 7-day money-back guarantee, no questions asked
**Promise:** In 14 days, you will build a working AI-powered career system: a tailored-resume engine, an application pipeline, an interview prep tool, and a daily AI workflow for your current job. Built, not planned.

**Who it is for:** Job seekers who are applying into a brutal market and getting silence, and corporate workers who know AI is reshaping their role and want to be the person who runs it, not the person replaced by it.

**Who it is not for:** People who want AI to do the thinking for them, or who want motivation without building anything.
